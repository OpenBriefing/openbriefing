The obs: namespace

Namespace: https://openbriefingstandard.org/ns#

These are the tags of the Open Briefing Standard, Draft 1.1. In a page they are <meta property="obs:…" content="…"> tags in the head. In a feed they are elements in this namespace. The specification is the authority; the tag names are not yet fixed.

TagMeaningRequired
obs:modifiedWhen any obs: tag on the page last changed, as an ISO 8601 date and timeyes
obs:summaryThe publisher's summary, of any length. Its tier comes from its length (§4).yes, unless a tiered summary is present
obs:summary:shortA summary written for the short tierno
obs:summary:mediumA summary written for the medium tierno
obs:summary:longA summary written for the long tierno
obs:titleA title for cards and lists, 12 words or fewerno
obs:headlineOne or two lines, 40 words or fewer, to tap or hear before going furtherno
obs:keepA line of 40 words or fewer that every form must preserve. The first is the core message (§6).no; up to 3
obs:highlightA passage of 60 words or fewer, taken word for word from the content, that may be quoted on its own (§7). Order is priority.no; up to 5
obs:highlight:labelA label of 6 words or fewer for the highlight it follows, such as "On control" (§7)no
obs:deriveWhat may be derived from the summary: verbatim, anchor or open (§5)no; default anchor
obs:derive:translateno asks that the summary not be translatedno; default yes
obs:derive:combineno asks that the summary not be merged with other sourcesno; default yes
obs:attributionHow the publisher wants to be credited, 12 words or fewer (§8)no
obs:preferencesummary or full (§11)no; default summary
obs:langThe language of the obs: text, as a BCP 47 tag. Defaults to the page's <html lang>.no
obs:urlThe canonical URL of the content the tags describe (§12)no
obs:versionThe major version of OBS the tags followno; default 1

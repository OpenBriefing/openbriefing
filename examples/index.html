Open Briefing examples

Synthetic pages carrying Open Briefing Standard tags, for hearing and checking a consumer against a real page.

  • full-set: It carries a headline, a short and a medium summary, three keep lines, three highlights (one with a label) and an attribution, so every length is the author's own words.
  • one-summary: It carries one summary, two keep lines and one highlight. A tool may cut shorter forms from that summary, but not write longer ones.
  • verbatim: It carries one summary marked verbatim and no highlights: a tool may play the summary as written or not at all, and may make no other form from it. Verbatim does not create highlights.
